How we help

Drawing on decades of facilities and engineering experience, data analytics, and AI-assisted decision support, we help leadership define the problem, weigh practical options, and decide with confidence.

1

Clarify the problem

Many facility issues get treated as isolated maintenance, energy, vendor, or capital problems. We work out what is actually happening, what matters most, and what needs to be addressed first.

2

Evaluate the options

We review proposals, assess vendor recommendations, compare scope, find the exclusions, and lay out the practical and financial implications before you commit.

3

Support the decision

From capital planning and RFP development to owner’s representation, we help leadership move forward with clearer documentation and less avoidable risk.

Why owner-side advisory matters

Facility and capital decisions are often shaped by vendors, contractors, internal pressures, budget limits, and incomplete information. Owners may know something needs to be done, but not always what should be done first, what it should really cost, or what risks are hiding in the proposal.

The Navitas Group works from the owner’s side of the table. We help clients ask better questions, challenge assumptions, compare options, and make informed decisions before money is committed.

      About The Navitas Group

      From the ideas of energy, movement, and informed navigation: helping owners and executives make better decisions about facility, energy, infrastructure, and capital challenges.

      Founded in 2006, The Navitas Group, LLC is an executive advisory firm specializing in facilities infrastructure, energy management, and capital decision support. We serve owners, executives, and operators in hospitality, healthcare, and commercial real estate: organizations where facilities decisions carry significant financial and operational consequences.

      We are seasoned operators, not theorists. Every recommendation we make is shaped by decades spent on the owner’s side of multimillion-dollar capital programs, energy portfolios, and mission-critical operations. We have sat in the room where these decisions get made, and we know what it takes to get them right.

      Our role is to navigate the complexity of the built environment on your behalf. As trusted advisors and owners’ representatives, we work exclusively for you, with no equipment to sell, no vendor relationships to protect, and no agenda beyond your success. When the stakes are high and the decisions are unfamiliar, we are the resource you turn to before you commit capital, sign a contract, or change direction.

      We’ll handle individual projects, but our model is built on long-term relationships, not one-off transactions. Over time, we become embedded in how you think about your facilities, your infrastructure, and your long-term capital plans: a senior partner you rely on to ask the right questions, see around corners, and bring clarity to decisions that will shape your organization for years to come.

      Founder & Managing Principal

      John J. Lembo, CFM, LEED AP

      John J. Lembo brings nearly four decades of experience in facilities management, energy, project management, and professional services. His career spans organizations from startups to Fortune 500 companies, with senior roles that have shaped how some of the world’s most recognized brands manage their infrastructure.

      As Senior Director of Energy & Sustainability at Starwood Hotels & Resorts, John oversaw energy programs across more than 200 full-service hotels in North America and managed a $1 billion outsourcing agreement, eventually taking it back in-house, subsequently earning the ENERGY STAR Partner of the Year recognition in both 2001 and 2002.

      As Vice President of Facilities at RiverSpring Living, he led a mission-critical healthcare campus through the dark days of COVID, maintaining uninterrupted care operations while transforming the physical environment.

      John has also served as Global Head of Facilities at WeWork, and Principal and Regional Vice President at TRC Companies, bringing executive-level leadership to each engagement. As an independent consultant, he performed facility assessments and energy audits for full-service hotels across the US and was engaged to develop the facilities and sustainability strategy for Highgate Hotels.

      He holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering from Pratt Institute and is a Certified Facility Manager (CFM), LEED Accredited Professional (LEED AP), and Certified Six Sigma Green Belt.

      John has been an Adjunct Professor at Manhattan University’s Graduate School of Civil Engineering and a Visiting Professor at Pratt Institute School of Architecture. He is the author of So You Want to Be a Consultant: Hard Truths, Real Tools and the No-BS Road to Consulting Success (2026), and is at work on his second book, In Layman’s Terms: The Complicated Business of Making Things Simple. He writes The Solution Navigator: Practical Thinking for Consultants, Leaders and Reluctant Entrepreneurs on Substack and publishes articles in FMJ, the publication of the International Facility Management Association.

      He founded The Navitas Group to offer the kind of independent, owner-side advisory that he spent his career wishing more clients had access to.
